Superadmission is built on systems that already exist. Using them needs **permission**, and some of the design rests on assumptions as of now.

## Permission to use what already exists

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="UIDAI approval for Aadhaar" icon="fingerprint">
    Using Aadhaar for identity checks needs an application to UIDAI, a technical review, and agreement to their terms of use.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DigiLocker, as a requester" icon="cloud-download">
    Pulling a student's documents from DigiLocker, even with consent, needs registration with the National Informatics Centre as an authorised requester.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A way to take payments" icon="indian-rupee">
    Collecting registration and acceptance fees needs a payment aggregator licence from the RBI, which is heavy, or a partnership with a bank or company that already has one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Truly DPDP-ready" icon="shield-check">
    Not only designed to comply, but reviewed and confirmed as compliant before handling real student data at scale.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The Assumptions behind the design

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Existing portals can be connected through APIs" icon="plug">
    The whole system depends on JoSAA, MCC and state CETs being able to offer a programmatic interface. If a portal cannot or will not, the fallback is manual data entry or screen-scraping. Both are far less reliable, and the benefit shrinks a lot.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Most document checking can be automated" icon="file-check">
    The vault assumes a computer can check most documents. If most still need a human look, the system needs a much bigger review team than planned, and the speed advantage shrinks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="DigiLocker has what is needed" icon="cloud-download">
    This assumes a meaningful share of documents are already there, especially state board marksheets and category certificates. Coverage varies by state. Where a document is missing, manual upload still works. It is just less of an improvement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Status can update in close to real time" icon="refresh-cw">
    The dashboard works well only if allotment status, payments and seat availability update quickly. If a counselling system updates once a day, the dashboard can show something stale. A clear last updated label helps, but it is a real limit.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What can be built right now

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="The profile and document layer" icon="user-check">

## What is assumed about a student

The design assumes a student has a smartphone or a computer with decent internet. It also assumes an Aadhaar number linked to a working mobile, since that is how identity is checked. Aadhaar itself is optional, and an alternate route exists.
