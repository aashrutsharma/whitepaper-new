<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which laws apply?" icon="scroll-text">
    The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the IT Act, 2000, and the Aadhaar Act, 2016. The DPDP Rules were notified in November 2025 and are being phased in. See [How Superadmission Handles Student Data](/superadmission/how-superadmission-handles-student-data).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is data stored?" icon="map-pin">
    On servers inside India.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What approvals are needed?" icon="stamp">
    UIDAI approval for Aadhaar checks. Registration with the National Informatics Centre as a DigiLocker requester. A payment aggregator licence from the RBI, or a partnership with a bank or company that already has one. A review confirming DPDP readiness. For a central authority, ministry clearance. See [Digital Infrastructure and Integrations](/superadmission/digital-infrastructure-and-integrations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does this fit national policy?" icon="landmark">
    It builds on Aadhaar, DigiLocker and APAAR, and borrows the shared-infrastructure idea from UPI and ONDC. PraveshAI is built to be inspected, which fits the focus on safe and trusted AI in the IndiaAI Mission. See [Superadmission as Digital Public Infrastructure](/superadmission/superadmission-as-digital-public-infrastructure).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How would a pilot work?" icon="route">
    A state-level pilot first. State systems are simpler than JoSAA or MCC, and talking to a state government is often faster. A pilot gives real numbers before central authorities are approached. Each new connection goes live only after its own pilot succeeds.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Superadmission replace a counselling authority?" icon="shield-check">
    No. The authority sets the rules, reviews the result and decides when to publish. A person reviews anything that could change whether a student gets a seat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is sensitive category data handled?" icon="lock">
    It is collected only where a seat's eligibility depends on it. It is never shared with a counselling body the student has not joined. The documents sit behind stricter access controls.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is still open?" icon="info">
    Whether Superadmission counts as a Significant Data Fiduciary. The legal basis for sharing a verified document with a counselling body. State-specific rules for category data. Who is liable if a verification is wrong. All four are listed openly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can decisions be audited?" icon="file-clock">
    Yes. Every meaningful action is recorded with what happened, who or what triggered it, and when. Automated decisions keep their inputs too. Records cannot be edited. A designated compliance officer can see the full log.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
