## Exams and scores

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Score" icon="hash">
    The marks you get in an exam. Example: 620 out of 720.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Percentile" icon="percent">
    Shows how many students you scored higher than. A 99 percentile means you did better than about 99 out of 100 students.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rank" icon="trophy">
    Your position in line. Rank 1 is the best. Rank is what counselling uses.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CRL (Common Rank List)" icon="list-ordered">
    The rank list of all students together, with no category split.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Category rank" icon="users">
    Your position among students of your own category.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cutoff" icon="scissors">
    The last rank that got a seat in a course.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Opening and closing rank" icon="arrow-up-down">
    The opening rank is the best rank that got a seat in a course. The closing rank is the last rank that got one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CET" icon="file-text">
    Common Entrance Test. Many states run their own for seats in that state.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Counselling words

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Counselling" icon="route">
    The process that matches a student's rank to a seat. It has nothing to do with therapy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Preference list (choice filling)" icon="list-ordered">
    Your list of colleges and courses, from most wanted to least wanted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Choice locking" icon="lock">
    Making your list final before the deadline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mock allotment" icon="eye">
    A practice result before the real one. It shows where you might land. It is not final.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Seat allotment" icon="badge-check">
    The seat the computer offers you in a round.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Round" icon="refresh-cw">
    One cycle of allotments. Free seats are offered again in the next round.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Freeze" icon="lock">
    Accept the seat and stop looking for a better one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Float" icon="arrow-up">
    Keep the seat for now. You can be moved up if a better choice on your list opens up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Slide" icon="arrow-right">
    Keep the college. You can be moved to a better course in the same college.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reporting" icon="building-2">
    Going to the college with your documents to confirm your seat.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Withdrawal" icon="log-out">
    Leaving the counselling process after you have taken part. Rules on refunds differ between counselling bodies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Spot round / Mop-up round" icon="timer">
    An extra round for seats left empty after the main rounds. The name and rules differ by counselling body.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stray vacancy round" icon="timer">
    A name used for the last rounds in medical counselling, for seats still empty.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deferred acceptance" icon="cpu">
    A matching method where offers are held, not final, until a round ends. A better offer can replace a weaker one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Seats and rules

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Seat matrix" icon="layout-grid">
    The full list of seats. It shows seats by college, course and category.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Intake" icon="users">
    The number of seats in a course.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reservation" icon="scale">
    Seats kept by rule for certain groups. Each group has its share of seats.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS, PwD" icon="users-round">
    SC: Scheduled Castes. ST: Scheduled Tribes. OBC-NCL: Other Backward Classes (non-creamy layer). EWS: Economically Weaker Sections. PwD: Persons with Disabilities.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Home State and Other State" icon="map-pin">
    Home State means the state where the college is. In NITs, half the seats go to Home State students and half to Other State students.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="All India Quota (AIQ)" icon="flag">
    A share of seats in state government medical colleges that students from any state can compete for.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Supernumerary seats" icon="plus">
    Extra seats added on top of the normal seats, so other students do not lose theirs. Example: seats added for women in IITs and NITs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Domicile" icon="house">
    The state where you officially live.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Organisations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="NTA" icon="file-text">
    National Testing Agency. Runs JEE Main, NEET UG, CUET UG and other exams.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="JoSAA" icon="cpu">
    Joint Seat Allocation Authority. Runs counselling for IITs, NITs, IIITs and other government funded technical institutes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CSAB" icon="refresh-cw">
    Central Seat Allocation Board. Runs extra rounds after JoSAA.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="MCC" icon="stethoscope">
    Medical Counselling Committee. Runs counselling for many MBBS and BDS seats.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="UGC" icon="scale">
    University Grants Commission. Looks after universities.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AICTE" icon="cpu">
    All India Council for Technical Education. Looks after technical education.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="NMC" icon="stethoscope">
    National Medical Commission. Looks after medical education.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AISHE" icon="database">
    All India Survey on Higher Education. The yearly government survey of colleges and universities.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="IIT, NIT, IIIT, GFTI" icon="graduation-cap">
    Indian Institute of Technology. National Institute of Technology. Indian Institute of Information Technology. Government Funded Technical Institute.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Digital words

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="NEP 2020" icon="landmark">
    National Education Policy 2020. India's plan for school and college education.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="GER" icon="percent">
    Gross Enrolment Ratio. About how many of every 100 young people aged 18 to 23 are in college.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Aadhaar" icon="fingerprint">
    A 12-digit identity number for every resident.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="DigiLocker" icon="lock">
    A safe online locker for official documents.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="APAAR" icon="user-check">
    A 12-digit lifelong ID for every student. Also called One Nation, One Student ID.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="ABC (Academic Bank of Credits)" icon="bookmark">
    A digital bank that stores a student's study credits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="DPDP Act" icon="shield-check">
    The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. India's law on how personal data can be used.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
