<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who sets the rules?" icon="list-checks">
    You do. Eligibility rules, reservation mandates, the seat matrix, and round timing and publication all stay with the authority. They are applied exactly as configured and never adjusted automatically.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the system publish results by itself?" icon="shield-check">
    No. The result comes to you for review first. Nothing reaches students until you authorise it. If something needs a fix, it is sent back.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fast can matching run?" icon="gauge">
    The same engine can process well over a million applications in under an hour.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What will my staff actually review?" icon="inbox">
    A short queue. Documents are scored and sorted first. Most clear automatically. Only uncertain ones come to a person, each flagged with what looks off.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What can we see during a round?" icon="activity">
    How many students are at each stage, how seats are filling by category and institute, and alerts. For example, a failed allocation run alerts you within roughly 60 seconds. See [Monitoring Admissions](/superadmission/monitoring-admissions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can we pause, extend, close or roll back?" icon="sliders-horizontal">
    Yes. Each is a deliberate action that is logged with who, when and why. Rollback needs more than one authorised person and a documented reason. See [Managing Admissions](/superadmission/managing-admissions).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do disputes reach the right person?" icon="route">
    A grievance is sorted by type the moment it is filed. Document disputes go to the document review team. Allocation disputes go to your designated reviewer. Then there are three levels of escalation, ending with your grievance officer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do we need to connect?" icon="plug">
    A technical review, usually by the National Informatics Centre, and for a central authority, ministry clearance. The system relies on your portal offering an API. If it cannot, the fallbacks are manual entry or screen-scraping, which are far less reliable. See [Digital Infrastructure and Integrations](/superadmission/digital-infrastructure-and-integrations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is participation optional?" icon="handshake">
    Yes. Counselling bodies are government agencies and nobody can force them. Participation has to be earned by showing clear benefits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who is responsible if a document check is wrong?" icon="scale">
    This is not settled yet. It needs legal analysis and possibly an insurance arrangement. It is listed as an open question on [How Superadmission Handles Student Data](/superadmission/how-superadmission-handles-student-data).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
