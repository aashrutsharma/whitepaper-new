Reading about an admission platform is one thing.

Experiencing how it works is another.

Welcome to the Superadmission Playground.

Here, you can explore interactive demonstrations of different parts of the platform.These demonstrations use sample student data, colleges and admission data to help you understand the workflows.

## How to use the Playground

1. Request playground access by adding your name and email address.
2. Once Access is granted; Enter Playground.
3. Follow the instructions on screen.
4. Experiment with different choices.
5. See how the system responds.

<Info>
  All demonstrations use simulated information unless explicitly stated otherwise. They do not submit real applications, allocate actual seats or change official admission records.
</Info>

## Why a Playground?

We want people to understand Superadmission by experiencing it.

Whether you are a student, parent, university administrator or government stakeholder, you should be able to explore the platform without needing a technical explanation.

**Explore. Experiment. Understand.**
