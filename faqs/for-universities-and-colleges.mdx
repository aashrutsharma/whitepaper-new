<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When does a college interact with Superadmission?" icon="school">
    Only in the later stages of admission, after counselling is over. There are two points of interaction with colleges. The first is when an allotment is confirmed. The second is reporting day, when the student comes to the college in person.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does a college receive when an allotment is confirmed?" icon="file-check">
    When a student's payment clears for a seat at the college, a record is sent to the college automatically. It shows the student's verified identity and personal information, the program, and the round of counselling in which the seat was allotted. It also shows the document status and the payment confirmation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does pre-verified mean?" icon="badge-check">
    It means the student's identity and other personal information have already been verified before the record reaches the college. The record also shows whether all documents are verified, and which documents the student used, such as Aadhaar, APAAR or another form of identity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do we know the seat payment has cleared?" icon="indian-rupee">
    The record includes a payment confirmation, which is proof that the seat payment has cleared. The record itself is sent to the college automatically at the moment the student's payment clears.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does reporting day work?" icon="qr-code">
    The student reports to the college in person. They carry a unique QR code that contains all their information and the required sign-in steps. College staff only need to scan this QR code to mark the student as reported.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if a student does not report?" icon="refresh-cw">
    If the QR code is never scanned, the seat is automatically treated as unclaimed. It is fed back into the system for the next round or the spot round. The college does not have to track this by hand or pass the information to the platform or the counselling authority.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the admission office have to track anything manually?" icon="list-checks">
    No. Unclaimed seats are handled by the system, so the admission office does not have to keep track of them. Student information also arrives in a structured form. This means less manual work, which is slow and prone to error.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does Superadmission make work easier for colleges?" icon="sparkles">
    Our aim is a better organised workflow for institutions. That means structured student information and other data, better operational visibility, and less manual workload. Manual work takes a lot of time and resources, and mistakes are easy to make.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Superadmission respect a college's own rules?" icon="handshake">
    Yes. Being institution-friendly is one of our guiding principles. We respect the role and rules of participating institutions. We are designing a system that works well for students and is good for institutions too.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do colleges appear on Superadmission?" icon="database">
    Colleges appear in Manifest, the repository of all colleges, universities and standalone institutions in India. It holds extensive verified information about each of them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

To learn more, read [How Colleges Use Superadmission](/superadmission/how-colleges-use-superadmission) and [Manifest](/superadmission/manifest).
