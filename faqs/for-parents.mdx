<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Superadmission, in simple words?" icon="layers">
    It is one place that sits above counselling systems like JoSAA and MCC. Your child keeps one profile, one set of verified documents and one dashboard, instead of repeating everything on many websites. It does not replace the counselling systems. See [What is Superadmission?](/superadmission/what-is-superadmission).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my child's data safe?" icon="shield-check">
    The system is built around India's data protection laws. Data is encrypted, stored on safe servers, and protected by access controls. Category and income documents have stricter access. Every action is recorded and the records cannot be edited. Details are on [Security and Privacy](/superadmission/security-and-privacy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who decides which seat my child gets?" icon="landmark">
    The counselling authority. It sets the rules, reviews the result and decides when to publish. Superadmission runs the process and the allocation algorithm based on respective rules.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can the system pick colleges for my child?" icon="lightbulb">
    No. It shows chances next to each choice. It never changes, reorders or locks in the list. Your child decides.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does my child still have to go to college in person?" icon="building-2">
    Yes. On reporting day your child goes with original documents. The college scans one code and checks payment, allotment and any duplicate admission in seconds.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if we think a result is wrong?" icon="scale">
    A grievance can be filed. It goes straight to the right reviewer: document review, the authority's reviewer, the college, or technical support. If it is not solved, it moves to a senior reviewer, then to the authority's grievance officer. You can see where it is and how long will the resolution take.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it replace a paid counsellor?" icon="user-check">
    Superadmission aims to give every student personalised, data-backed advice, for free. It is guidance accessible to all at each step during the admission. The final choice and every real action stay with the student.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to the documents after admission?" icon="archive">
    Profile and documents are kept for the cycle plus one year, in case of a dispute. Allotment records and audit logs are kept for 7 years. A student can ask for deletion and it is done within 30 days. See [How Superadmission Handles Student Data](/superadmission/how-superadmission-handles-student-data).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
