## Our Mission

To simplify and connect higher education admissions through a unified digital platform that brings students, institutions and admission authorities closer together.

## Our Vision

A future where every student can navigate the journey to higher education through a simpler, more transparent and connected admission experience.

## Our guiding principles

- **Student-first:** Build around the needs of people navigating admissions.
- **Institution-friendly:** Respect the role and rules of participating institutions.
- **Interoperable:** Support connections with appropriate digital systems.
- **Transparent:** Make processes and information easier to understand.
- **Responsible:** Treat student data and institutional information carefully.
- **Scalable:** Build with the diversity and size of India's education system in mind.

> Our mission is the work we are doing today. Our vision is the future we want to help create.
