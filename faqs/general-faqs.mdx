<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What are the three parts?" icon="blocks">
    Manifest, a repository of India's higher education institutions. Superadmission, the platform a student uses for counselling and admission. PraveshAI, the thinking layer underneath that provides personalised guidance to students.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it replace JoSAA or MCC?" icon="landmark">
    No. It sits alongside them. The authority sets the rules, reviews the result and decides when to publish while superadmission acts as the host for these processes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is PraveshAI a separate app?" icon="cpu">
    No. It is the part of Superadmission that does the thinking and guidance during admission process inside the superadmission dashboard. See [PraveshAI](/superadmission/praveshai).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What can PraveshAI not decide on its own?" icon="shield-check">
    Pravesh AI can only guide and assist students. It cannot publish an allotment. It cannot resolve a grievance. Any significant action has to be approved by a human(in case of students they use their security PIN for approval).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does verified mean in Manifest?" icon="badge-check">
    A fact traces back to a government source or a clearly disclosed method. It does not mean a person checked every field by hand. A blank field means not verified yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can colleges pay to rank higher in Manifest?" icon="ban">
    No. Rankings come from a government metric or a disclosed method. Manifest does not charge institutions to be listed or ranked. Manifest is a neutral, unbiased repository.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is some Manifest data missing?" icon="database">
    Government data does not cover everything. The rest is read from each institution's own website, and that works for roughly 40 to 60 percent of institutions on a given field. For NIRF-ranked institutions it works above 85 percent. See [Manifest](/superadmission/manifest).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my data safe?" icon="lock">
    Superadmission takes utmost care of security and data safety. Read [Trust and Security](/superadmission/trust-and-security) to know more
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I start if admissions are new to me?" icon="book-open">
    Start with [How Admissions in India Actually Work](/understanding-admissions/how-admissions-in-india-actually-work), then [What is Counselling?](/understanding-admissions/what-is-counselling).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
