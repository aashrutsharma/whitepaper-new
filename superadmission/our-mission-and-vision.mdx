We want merit to be the sole decider of any admission granted to any student. A lack of accessibility or proper guidance at the right time or unawarness about how to navigate different platforms, and technical challenges should not become a barrier to admission for any student, whatever walk of life they come from.

## What we have seen

We have seen first-hand how students lose deserved admissions despite their merit, because of technical challenges or a lack of guidance. In India, a food delivery app handles its traffic better than a national admissions website. Admission websites crash regularly. Every important alert is posted on a cluttered website instead of being delivered directly to students.

The system works, but it has many bottlenecks, and we aim to erradicate them.

## Our vision

We aim to create a future where every student can navigate the journey to higher education through a simple, transparent and connected admission experience. Students should not have to rely on any external resource or unverified information to make decisions that change their lives.

## Our guiding principles

1. **Student first.** We build Superadmission mainly around the people navigating admission: students.
2. **Institution-friendly.** We respect the role and rules of participating institutions. We are designing a system that is beneficial for students and efficient for institutions too.
3. **Transparent.** Everything that happens should be visible and understandable to both students and authorities.
4. **Responsible and secure.** We treat student data and other sensitive information with the utmost care and security. 
5. **Scalable.** We are building Superadmission to accommodate the diversity of India: educational streams, languages, types of institutions and everything in between.
