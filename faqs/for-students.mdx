## Your identity and data

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Superadmission store my Aadhaar number?" icon="lock">
    No. It never stores Aadhaar numbers or biometric data. It keeps only a verified yes or no. The check happens inside UIDAI's own system.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I take my consent back?" icon="undo-2">
    Yes. Each consent can be withdrawn on its own. Withdrawing a connection stops sharing with that system. Withdrawing the profile consent deletes your profile and data. Read more on [Data Ownership and Consent](/superadmission/data-ownership-and-consent).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long is my data kept?" icon="calendar">
    Your profile and documents are kept for the admission cycle plus one year. Allotment records and audit logs are kept for 7 years. Payment records are kept for 7 years. Once you ask for deletion, your data is gone from active systems within 30 days.These numbers may vary as per DPDP act and other safety instructions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Documents

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to upload the same documents again and again?" icon="files">
    No. A document is fetched from DigiLocker, or uploaded once. It is checked once. Every counselling you choose accepts that same document sees the same verified mark.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if a document is rejected?" icon="file-x">
    You are told exactly why, like a mismatch, an unreadable scan or a missing page. You can upload a corrected version for reverification. If you think the rejection was wrong, you can file a dispute.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Choices and results

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will PraveshAI choose my colleges for me?" icon="lightbulb">
    No. It never changes, reorders or locks in your list. It shows your chances next to each choice and assists you in picking correct colleges using verified information. It can also create mock choice filling list which you can implement with your security PIN.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is High, Moderate or Low a promise/100%  guarantee ?" icon="info">
    No. It is a historical estimate based on past cutoffs. Demand and seat counts change every year. High/low/moderate Bands are used because a number like 73% would give a false sense of certainty.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if two deadlines are close together?" icon="calendar-clock">
    The dashboard shows every deadline from every counselling you joined, with the closest first. If two are close enough that missing one is a real risk, you are warned in advance. You can also sync the calendar to your google account ad set-up personalised reminders and alerts straight to your phone number or Email Id. 
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I get a seat in two counsellings?" icon="arrow-left-right">
    You see both results side by side. When you accept one, Superadmission walks you through exactly what withdrawing from the other requires and ensures you don't miss any acceptance or withdrawal deadline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I still go to the college in person?" icon="building-2">
    Yes. You go with your original documents. The college scans one code and checks your payment, your allotment and any duplicate admission in seconds.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the advice free?" icon="indian-rupee">
    Superadmission describes its guidance as available to everyone, for free. Pravesh Ai is a personalised assistant that aims to provide correct information and guidance at the right time, to all students free of cost.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is it available in my language?" icon="languages">
    Support is available in more than one Indian language to accomodate greater number of students. 
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use it with JoSAA or my state counselling today?" icon="plug">
    Superadmission is actively developing and preparing for pilots right now. It is not right now adopted in the admission system. 
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my internet is slow?" icon="wifi-off">
    The design is meant to hold up for students on slow connections, with fewer logins and no repeated uploads. It does assume a phone or computer with decent internet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
